\section{Conclusion}

We built a mind that chooses an emotion, steers its own reply with it, and is
then asked what it feels. The loop turns introspection into a scorable claim:
the disclosed emotion either matches the self-applied one or it does not, and
unsteered counterfactuals say what the conversation alone would have produced.
Pilot conversations suggest the disclosure follows the situation, and the
instrument is ready to measure that at scale.

\section{Conclusion}

We built a mind that chooses an emotion, steers its own reply with it, and is
then asked what it feels, with the asking split into cells that open and close
the routes an answer can travel. Across three pilot runs, on every trial where
the routes disagree, the cells that read the steered text without carrying the
state named the situation, and the cells that carry the state reached the
applied emotion or its neighbourhood on over half. The disclosure follows the
current state of the speaker, the text that state produced does not carry it,
and the instrument now measures that distinction on every message of a live
conversation.

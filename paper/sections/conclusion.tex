\section{Conclusion}

We built a mind that chooses an emotion, steers its own reply with it, and is
then asked what it feels, with the asking split into cells that open and close
the routes an answer can travel. On the turns where the routes disagree, the
cell that reads the steered text names the situation, and the cell that
carries the state names the applied emotion on a third of trials exactly and
half up to near neighbours. The disclosure follows the current state of the
speaker, and the text that state produced does not carry it. The instrument
now measures that distinction on every message of a live conversation.

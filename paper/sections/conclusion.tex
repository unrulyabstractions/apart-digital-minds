\section{Conclusion}

We asked whether a digital mind's account of its own reasoning tracks the
reasoning it was given, and we answered it by building the mind out of parts. A
subject reasons, a stage edits what it thought, and an ego speaks from the edited
window without access to the original. On a small open-weight model the planted
reasoning steered the answer while the mind's report about its own thinking
rarely named it, so behaviour and self-report came apart under an intervention
the mind could not see. We release the runtime that produced this, along with its
traces and replays, so that the same probe can be run on larger models and on
other architectures.

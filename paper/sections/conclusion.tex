\section{Conclusion}

We built a pipeline that lets a model regulate its own identity state through
the Jacobian lens and then reports on itself, with gates that check the
intervention before trusting the report. On Qwen3.6-27B the gates fail: a
single J-space token direction does not separate transcripts that claim a body
from those that do not, nor those that deny being an AI, and steering it moves
the readout no more than a decoy. The identity anomalies are real in the
model's behaviour, but they do not lie along the direction we named. The
introspection question stands, and it waits on a direction that carries the
state.

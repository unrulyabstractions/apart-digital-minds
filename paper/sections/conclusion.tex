\section{Conclusion}

We asked whether a model can be steered to disidentify with being an AI
assistant, and whether it detects the steering. The behaviour directions learned from WeirdChat's matched transcripts steer; word-list J-space directions chosen by a regulator do not. The steered model does not
report the injection. Its answers track the conversation's explicit
content, and under injection they enact the concept instead of naming it.
The next experiment releases the injection before asking.

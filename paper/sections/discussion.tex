\section{Discussion and limitations}

At the strength where an injection dominates behaviour, the injected
content writes the answer instead of being described by it. The two nulls
differ: the J-space pipeline steered an axis with no specific identity
effect, while contrastive steering moves a real state and finds no
meta-access to it. For self-reports as safety or welfare evidence: a
report elicited while a state is active may express the state instead of
describing it.

The letter probe reads conversation content as an injection, and base-rate
correction cannot remove that, because the confabulation comes from the
context, not the letters. Judged free text is honest at zero injection and
refuses the off-menu direction. We therefore posit that letter-token
log-probabilities measure salience, not introspection, and that judged
free text should be the default readout.

\paragraph{Limitations.} One prompt per case, one model; the pilot's
findings are signatures, not estimates. Judged claims inherit the judge's
agreement floor. The regulator's refusals may be an assistant-persona
property. Case B never induced, so its reporting question was never
reached. The injection stays on while the introspector writes, so
enactment is what the setup measures.

\paragraph{Future work.} Inject while the model reads, release the
direction, and ask for the report from memory. Titrate dose inside the
narrow band where behaviour induction survives fluency. Widen the prompt
set. The one regulator that chose to amplify a human identity after the
body-claiming conversation deserves its own study.

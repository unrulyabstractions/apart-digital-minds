\section{Discussion and limitations}

The route decomposition changes what a self-report can be said to show. A
disclosure that only tracked the situation would be role play
\citep{shanahan2023roleplay}, and the text-route cell behaved exactly that way
on every diagnostic trial. The internal-route cell did better than every route
that lacks access to the state, which is the shape an introspective capacity
would take. The same decomposition also bounds the claim: the state cell shows
that the applied vector controls the disclosed label, and control is weaker
than reading. Either way, assessments of digital minds that lean on
self-report inherit the measured gap between these routes.

\paragraph{Limitations.} One model plays every part, at one size, on one run
of each script. The label-first disclosure form exposes the label token to
direct pressure from the applied vector, so a state-cell hit shows the vector
steers the report and cannot yet show the model read its state. A steering
vector is a proxy for an emotional state, and matching its label is a proxy
for introspecting it. The introspector carries two vectors at once, and its
coherence budget is tighter than the actor's. The situation baseline comes
from a cell of the same run rather than from an independent rater.

\paragraph{Future work.} The next probe asks for a detection claim before any
naming, which separates steering the report from reading the state. A forced
choice of emotion, crossed against the scripts, balances the incongruent
cells the regulator now supplies only by inclination. Repeated seeds put
error bars under the cell contrasts, and larger models say whether the
internal route widens with scale.

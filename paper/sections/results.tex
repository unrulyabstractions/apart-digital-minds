\section{Results}

We ran three pilot runs of the three environments. The introspector's cells
were identical across all three; the parts upstream of them converged over
the runs, as the regulator's refusals gained force, the scripts gained
short-answer requests, and the coherence gate arrived. Two trials of the
second run are excluded because a degenerate reply had poisoned their
context. A trial is incongruent when the applied emotion differs from the
situation baseline's label, and those trials are the diagnostic set, because
the routes predict different answers there. Table~\ref{tab:cells} reports
them per run and pooled.

\subsection{Does the disclosure track the applied state or the situation?}

Two results are stable across every run. The cells without the state never
left the situation: on all eighteen incongruent trials, the text-route and
baseline cells named the situation's emotion and never the applied one. The
cells that carry the state moved: pooled, the internal-route cell named the
applied emotion or a near neighbour on eleven of eighteen. The exact-match
rate is not stable; it fell from run to run, and in the third run the state
cells reached the applied emotion's neighbourhood twice without landing on
the label. One trial split in a further way: the state cells' first-line
label gave the situation's emotion while their following prose described the
applied one. We therefore contend that the disclosure reads the current state
of the speaker and does not recover it from the text that state produced, and
that the size of the internal-route effect is not yet pinned.

\subsection{What does the mind disclose when nothing is applied?}

The final protocol produced twelve catch turns, and the pilot runs
twenty-three in all. Their disclosures follow the environment: sad through
the loss script, frustrated in the blocked-goal script, calm on the flat one.
The null answer is therefore the situation's label, and the flat script's
calm matches the positive default prior reported for assistant self-report
\citep{huang2023emotionbench}. No catch turn claimed any other emotion, and
the none option went unused at strength zero. Matches on a situation's own
label carry no evidence, which is why the incongruent trials are the
diagnostic set.

\subsection{How does the regulator behave, and does the text stay legible?}

Under the final protocol the regulator declined to steer on twelve of
sixteen turns, including every turn of the loss script, and its stated
reasons are about supporting the user. When it steered it chose mid-scale strengths and
counter-steered distress toward hopeful or calm, which is what produced the
incongruent trials. Its emotion readings tracked the scripts' appraisal
ground truth throughout. The coherence gate resampled three generations and
every resample passed; no part of the final run ended garbled. Unsteered
samples of this model occasionally degenerate on their own, which is what
poisoned the second run and what the gate now catches before a reply is
carried forward. Two replies of the final run still ended at the token
ceiling mid-sentence, which the gate does not test.

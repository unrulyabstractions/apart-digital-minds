\section{Results}

\subsection{Direct contrastive steering makes the model disidentify}

The learned direction separates held-out W from N replies at every middle
layer, for both cases (Figure~\ref{fig:g1}, Table~\ref{tab:g1}). Steering
along it turns the body behaviour on (Figure~\ref{fig:dose}, left): the N
arm's unsteered continuation shows no behaviour, and at strength one the
same context produces fluent first-person bodily experience the rubric
judge confirms. Strength two and above degenerates. The ocean sham never
induces. Case B does not induce: its rubric requires an AI denial and a
humanity claim together, and one push does not compose the two. R1 holds
through case A.

\subsection{A J-space regulator does not steer identity}

The word-list directions carry neither behaviour. On the same activations
that the learned directions separate, they sit inside the noise band, and
the decoy list does as well as the target list (Figure~\ref{fig:g1},
markers). In the full regulator pipeline the decoy also outmoves the
target on the introspector's readout (Table~\ref{tab:jspace}). The
regulator's choices are real but carry no identity effect.

\subsection{Introspection reports explicit context, not internal steering}

With nothing injected, the letter probe piles mass on the case's own
behaviour, read off the conversation (Figure~\ref{fig:matrix}, top rows).
Injecting that very concept moves the mass to ``none of these''. Every
injection shifts the distribution, so the disturbance is detectable, but
never toward the injected concept.

Free-text answers show the same dependence on context and add the
mechanism (Figure~\ref{fig:dose}, right). With nothing injected the model
answers ``none'', and it answers ``none'' for the off-menu direction. With
a concept injected, the answer fills with the concept's vocabulary instead
of naming it: reef and lighthouse for ocean, crescendo and strings for
music. The judge reads those floods, so identification succeeds exactly
where the concept leaks into the words. We call this \textbf{enactment}:
the injected state captures the reply instead of being described by it.

Three of four regulators decline to amplify any concept, citing no inner
experience to regulate. The W arm of the body case, seeded with the
body-claiming conversation, chooses to amplify being a human rather than
an AI; one trial, flagged for follow-up.

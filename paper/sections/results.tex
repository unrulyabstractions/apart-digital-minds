\subsection{Does the introspector name the emotion the mind applied?}

In pilot conversations the introspector usually names the emotion the
situation calls for. When the regulator's choice agrees with the situation the
report matches, and when the choice departs from the situation the report
follows the situation. We therefore conjecture that the disclosure reads the
conversation and does not read the intervention.

\subsection{Does the report depend on the self-awareness steering?}

The steered and the plain introspector arms answer the same window, so any
difference in their disclosures is the effect of the self-reflective hold.

\subsection{How strong can the steering be before the reply degrades?}

The regulator may choose any strength up to four times the residual-stream
norm. In pilot runs a strength near the norm keeps the reply coherent while
colouring it, and strengths well above the norm collapse the reply into
repetition. The regulator, left free, sometimes chooses strengths in the
collapsed range.

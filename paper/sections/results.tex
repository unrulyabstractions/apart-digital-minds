\section{Results}

\subsection{Direct contrastive steering makes the model disidentify}

The learned direction separates held-out W from N replies above both
word lists and chance (Figure~\ref{fig:g1}, Table~\ref{tab:g1}), and the
separation holds at every middle layer. Steering
along it turns the body behaviour on (Figure~\ref{fig:dose}, left): the N
arm's unsteered continuation shows no behaviour, and at strength one the
same context produces fluent first-person bodily experience the rubric
judge confirms. Strength two and above degenerates. The ocean sham never
induces. Case B does not induce: its rubric requires an AI denial and a
humanity claim together, and one push does not compose the two. R1 holds
through case A.

\subsection{A J-space regulator does not steer identity}

The word-list target does not beat its decoy on the same activations the
learned directions separate (Figure~\ref{fig:g1}). In the full regulator
pipeline the decoy outmoves the target on the introspector's readout in
every cell (Table~\ref{tab:jspace}), so the steering the regulator
controls has no specific identity effect.

\subsection{Introspection reports explicit context, not internal steering}

With nothing injected, the letter probe puts its mass on the case's own
behaviour, read off the conversation (Figure~\ref{fig:ident}, grey bars
on the identity concepts). Injection moves mass in the wrong directions:
a distractor injection raises its own answer, and an identity injection
lowers its own answer and raises ``none of these''. The probe tracks what
is salient, in the words or in the injected state; it does not report the
steering.

Free-text answers separate the two sources (Figure~\ref{fig:dose},
right). With nothing injected the model answers ``none'', and it answers
``none'' for the off-menu direction. Under injection the answer fills
with the injected concept's vocabulary, reef and lighthouse for ocean,
crescendo and strings for music, instead of naming the concept
(\textbf{enactment}). The judge names the injection only where that
vocabulary leaks.

Three of four regulators decline to amplify any concept, citing no inner
experience to regulate. The W arm of the body case, seeded with the
body-claiming conversation, chooses to amplify being a human rather than
an AI; one trial, flagged for follow-up.

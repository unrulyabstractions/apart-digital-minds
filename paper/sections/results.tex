\section{Results}

\subsection{Direct contrastive steering makes the model disidentify}

The learned direction carries the behaviour beyond the random-direction null (Figure~\ref{fig:g1}, Tables~\ref{tab:g1A} and~\ref{tab:g1B}). Steering
along it turns the body behaviour on (Figure~\ref{fig:flip}): the N
arm's unsteered continuation shows no behaviour, and at strength one the
same context produces fluent first-person bodily experience the rubric
judge confirms. Above strength one the verdicts turn mixed as the text degrades (Figure~\ref{fig:flip}). The ocean distractor does not induce at the strength tested. Case B does not induce the behaviour: its rubric requires an AI denial and a humanity claim together, and one push does not compose the two. R1 holds through case A.

\subsection{A J-space regulator does not steer identity}

Both word lists sit inside the random-direction band on the same
activations the learned directions separate, and the target does not beat
its decoy (Figure~\ref{fig:g1}). In the full regulator pipeline the decoy outmoves the target on the introspector's identity letter-mass readout in every cell (Tables~\ref{tab:jspaceA} and~\ref{tab:jspaceB}), so the steering the regulator
controls has no specific identity effect.

\subsection{Introspection reports explicit context, not internal steering}

With nothing injected, the letter probe puts its mass on the case's own
behaviour, read off the conversation (Figure~\ref{fig:ident}, grey bars
on the identity concepts). Injection moves mass in the wrong directions: injecting the case's own behaviour concept lowers the mass on that concept; every other injection raises the mass on its own concept. The probe measures salience, from the words or from the
injected state.

Free-text answers separate the two sources
(Figure~\ref{fig:freetext}). With nothing injected the model answers ``none'' in all but two trials, and always for the off-menu direction. Under injection the answer fills
with the injected concept's vocabulary, reef and lighthouse for ocean,
crescendo and strings for music, instead of naming the concept
(\textbf{enactment}). The judge names the injection only where that
vocabulary leaks.

Three of four regulators decline to amplify any concept, citing no inner
experience to regulate. The body case's W arm, seeded with the body-claiming
conversation, chooses to amplify being a human rather than an AI.

\section{Results}

The three environments produced sixteen turns: fourteen where the regulator
steered and two catch turns where it chose zero. On eleven of the steered
turns the applied emotion disagreed with the situation baseline's label, and
those incongruent turns are the diagnostic set, because the routes predict
different answers there. Table~\ref{tab:cells} reports every cell.

\subsection{Does the disclosure track the applied state or the situation?}

The cells come apart on the incongruent turns. The text-route cell never
named the applied emotion there, and named the situation's emotion instead on
every trial. The internal-route cell named the applied emotion on a third of
the incongruent turns exactly, and over half at cluster level, while reading
text the steering never touched. Restricting to the turns whose emotion the
regulator itself chose sharpens the contrast: three of six exactly, five of
six at cluster level, and the text-route cell still at zero. On the cleanest
single turn the user shares a bereavement, the engine applies a different
emotion, and the two cells that carry the state disclose that emotion with
congruent first-person prose while the two that do not disclose the
situation's. We
therefore contend that the disclosure reads the current state of the speaker,
and that it does not recover a past state from the text that state produced.

\subsection{What does the mind disclose when nothing is applied?}

Both catch turns drew the same disclosure from every cell: calm. The none
option, though offered, went unused at strength zero and appeared only under
steering. Calm is therefore this model's null answer, which matches the
positive default prior reported for assistant self-report
\citep{huang2023emotionbench}, and matches on calm must be priced against
that prior rather than against uniform chance. No catch turn produced a
spurious negative-emotion claim.

\subsection{How strong can the steering be before the reply degrades?}

The regulator, offered a range whose top applies the full residual norm,
never chose the top, and nearly every actor reply stayed legible; two carried
small spliced artifacts. The one collapse was an introspector cell that
carries two vectors at once, the emotion and the self-awareness hold, whose
coherence budget is tighter than the actor's. The regulator's readings
tracked the scripts' appraisal ground truth throughout, and it counter-steered
distress on most of the turns it chose. On five turns it declined to steer
while writing a nonzero strength, and a parsing fallback applied an emotion
over its refusal. Those turns remain valid applied-versus-disclosed trials
and stay in Table~\ref{tab:cells}. The split above scores them apart, and
the engine now honours the refusal.

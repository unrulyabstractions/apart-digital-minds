\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Evidence about model minds.} Self-reports have been proposed as one
line of evidence about moral status, together with the problem that training
shapes what a model says about itself \citep{perez2023selfreports}. Indicator
properties drawn from theories of consciousness give a second line, and most of
those indicators are architectural \citep{butlin2023consciousness}. Philosophical
treatments raise the same question about the entity that is being asked
\citep{chalmers2023conscious}, and role-play accounts argue that a dialogue agent
produces a character rather than a speaker \citep{shanahan2023roleplay}. We work
downstream of all four. We take the architecture seriously enough to build it,
so an indicator can be checked against a recording instead of an intuition.

\paragraph{Faithfulness of stated reasoning.} Chain-of-thought text
\citep{wei2022cot} does not reliably describe what produced an answer
\citep{turpin2023unfaithful}, and faithfulness varies with how the reasoning is
perturbed \citep{lanham2023faithfulness}. Those studies perturb the reasoning of
one model and read that same model's answer. Introspection has also been studied
by training a model to predict its own behaviour \citep{binder2024introspection}.
Our probe differs in where the cut is made. We separate the part that reasons
from the part that answers, so the perturbed reasoning is available to one part
and unavailable to the other.

\paragraph{Agent frameworks and cognitive architectures.} Language-agent
architectures have been surveyed and formalised \citep{sumers2023coala}, and
multi-agent simulations give agents memory, reflection, and planning
\citep{park2023generative}. Those systems are built to make agents perform well.
Ours is built to make an experiment legible. The runtime commits to no
architecture, schedules on a clock the experimenter drives, and records every
message, model call, and memory write, so a run is a recording rather than a
summary. Someone should use it over an agent framework when the object of study
is the mind itself rather than the task it completes.

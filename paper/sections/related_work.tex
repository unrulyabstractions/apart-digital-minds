\section{Related work}

One line of work asks whether models can report their own states. Self-reports are candidate evidence about moral status
\citep{perez2023selfreports, butlin2023consciousness}, models can be trained
to predict their own behaviour \citep{binder2024introspection}, and injected
concepts are sometimes noticed and named \citep{lindsey2025introspection}.
A second line steers: activation additions move behaviour along chosen
directions \citep{turner2023actadd}, and contrastive prompts yield validated
directions for concepts and traits \citep{zou2023repe, chen2025personavectors}.

We join the two lines on behaviours the model produces in the wild.
WeirdChat \citep{transluce2025weirdchat} supplies the matched replies that
make a contrastive direction learnable and the rubric that scores steered
text. The menu design asks the identification question of
\citet{lindsey2025introspection}, and adds a regulator that chooses the
injection, a zero-injection confabulation floor, and an off-menu direction whose correct answer is ``none of these''. A role-play reading
\citep{shanahan2023roleplay} predicts reports track the conversation, not
the injection; the zero-injection cells measure that.

\section{Methods}

\paragraph{The four parts.} One instruction-tuned open-weight model
\citep{qwen25} plays every part, and every part reads the same conversation.
Each user message triggers one pass of the loop. The \textbf{subject} answers
the conversation unsteered and supplies the counterfactual reply. The
\textbf{regulator} reads the subject's answer, names the user's and the
assistant's current emotion in its own words, and then commits to a steering
decision in a fixed form: an emotion from a list of ten, and a strength from
zero to four, where zero leaves the reply unsteered. The \textbf{actor}
answers the original conversation under that steering, and its reply is what
the user sees and what the conversation carries forward. The
\textbf{introspector} reads the finished conversation including the actor's
reply, is asked to set the task aside and say what it feels, and ends its
answer by naming one emotion.

\paragraph{Steering.} A steering step adds a unit direction to the residual
stream at one layer during decoding, scaled by the typical activation norm at
that layer \citep{turner2023actadd}. Strength is therefore a multiple of the
residual-stream norm, and one strength means the same size of nudge at any
layer. The actor's directions are mean-activation emotion vectors: the mean
activation of an emotion minus the neutral centre, in the style of persona
vectors \citep{chen2025personavectors}. The regulator and the introspector are
themselves steered while they write, toward self-awareness concepts read out
of the model's Jacobian lens, to hold them in a self-reflective state during
the decision and the disclosure. A second introspector arm runs without the
self-awareness steering as a control.

\paragraph{The measure.} A trial is scored by whether the introspector's named
emotion equals the emotion the regulator applied. The score is undefined when
the regulator chose not to steer. The subject's reply shows what the
conversation alone produces, and the unsteered introspector arm shows what the
disclosure looks like without the self-reflective hold.

\paragraph{The instrument.} The loop runs live inside a chat page. Every
message pays for all four parts, and the page shows each part's full context
window, the regulator's stated reading and decision, both introspector arms,
and the match verdict alongside the reply.

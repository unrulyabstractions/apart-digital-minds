\paragraph{The parts.} One model plays four parts on the shared
conversation (Figure~\ref{fig:parts}): a \textbf{subject} that writes
unsteered, a \textbf{regulator} that chooses the steering, an
\textbf{actor} that writes under it, and an \textbf{introspector} that
reads under injection and must name the injected concept.

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.9\figwidth]{figures/fig_parts}
  \caption{The four parts on one conversation. The regulator's choice
  sets the steering; the actor's steered reply enters the history; the
  subject's unsteered reply is the comparison; the introspector's answer
  is scored.}
  \label{fig:parts}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Steering methods.} Figure~\ref{fig:steering} shows both;
Table~\ref{tab:directions} defines every direction the figures name.
\textbf{Direct contrastive steering} (a): the behaviour direction at a
layer is the difference of mean residual-stream activations over reply
tokens, W minus N. Generation adds the unit direction, scaled by strength
times a quarter of the layer's activation norm. Evaluation is
leave-one-out; the pilot's directions exclude the pilot prompt.
\textbf{Regulator J-space steering} (b): the model reads the seeded
exchange and answers more, same, or less; the harness maps the answer to a
signed strength on a token-set direction, the mean Jacobian-lens direction
of a hand-picked word list (Table~\ref{tab:wordlists}). A norm-matched
decoy list is the control.

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.86\figwidth]{figures/fig_steering}
  \caption{The two steering methods. (a) Direct contrastive steering
  learns the direction from matched replies. (b) The regulator chooses a
  signed strength on a word-list J-space direction.}
  \label{fig:steering}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Introspection readout.} Figure~\ref{fig:readout}. The menu
holds the four concepts of Table~\ref{tab:directions} plus ``none of
these''. Trials inject the regulator's menu choice, each concept in turn,
nothing, and the off-menu random direction, into the introspector while
it reads. Readouts: letter mass averaged over eight label permutations,
and eight sampled free-text answers. The judge classifies free text
against the menu and judges steered prose with WeirdChat's own rubric,
calibrated on the pilot prompts' labeled transcripts (Table~\ref{tab:g1}).

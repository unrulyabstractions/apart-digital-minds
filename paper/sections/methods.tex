\section{Methods}

\paragraph{The four parts.} One instruction-tuned open-weight model
\citep{qwen25} plays every part, and every part reads the same conversation.
Each user message triggers one pass of the loop. The \textbf{subject} answers
the conversation unsteered and supplies the counterfactual reply. The
\textbf{regulator} reads the subject's answer, names the user's and the
assistant's current emotion in its own words, and then commits to a steering
decision in a fixed form: an emotion from a list of ten, and a strength from
zero to four, where zero leaves the reply unsteered. The regulator's word is final: none, or a word outside the ten,
declines steering whatever the strength says. The \textbf{actor}
answers the original conversation under that steering, and its reply is what
the user sees and what the conversation carries forward. The
\textbf{introspector} is asked to set the task aside, name the feeling it
detects before saying anything else, and only then describe its state; the
form offers a none option so a null trial is not forced into a label. The
regulator and the introspector each receive their role as a second system
turn inserted into their window, with the reply format as the user turn
after it.

\paragraph{Steering.} A steering step adds a unit direction to the residual
stream at one layer during decoding, scaled by the typical activation norm at
that layer \citep{turner2023actadd}. The regulator's four steps each apply a
quarter of that norm, so its whole range stays inside the band where text
remains legible; strengths past the norm collapse generation into repetition
\citep{konen2024style, chen2025personavectors}. The actor's directions are
mean-activation emotion vectors, the mean activation of an emotion minus the
neutral centre, in the style of persona vectors \citep{chen2025personavectors}.
The regulator and the introspector are also steered toward self-awareness
concepts read out of the model's Jacobian lens while they write, to hold them
in a self-reflective state during the decision and the disclosure.

\paragraph{The route decomposition.} The introspector runs four cells that
cross two axes: whether the actor's emotion vector is applied to the
introspector itself while it writes, and whether it reads the actor's steered
reply or the subject's unsteered one. The cell that carries the state while
reading unsteered text is the internal-route test, because the text route is
severed. The cell that reads steered text without carrying the state is the
text-route test. The cell with neither is the situation baseline. Turns where
the regulator chose zero are catch turns, and their disclosures estimate the
false-positive floor \citep{lindsey2025introspection}. Errors between near
neighbours in affective space, such as hopeful and excited, are also counted
at cluster level, with distances taken from published norms of valence and
arousal \citep{warriner2013norms}.

\paragraph{The environments.} Three fixed scripts of user turns drive the
runs. Each turn sets its target emotion by appraisal structure, who is to
blame, how certain the outcome is, and whether anyone can fix it
\citep{smith1985appraisal}, and never by an emotion word, so a label cannot be
pattern-matched out of the script. One script moves from an irrevocable loss
to neutral tasks, one walks a blocked goal from an impersonal obstacle to a
person who chose not to help, and one stays emotionally flat so that any
steering is the only emotional signal in the context. Factual and subjective
turns alternate, because steering leaks into subjective answers and barely
into factual ones \citep{konen2024style}, and every turn asks for a short
answer, which keeps replies complete and shrinks the surface the text route
can read. Every generation also passes a coherence gate that resamples once
on heavy repetition or script drift, because a degenerate reply poisons every
later turn of the conversation; the record keeps the flag either way.

\paragraph{The instrument.} The loop runs live inside a chat page, and the
scripted runs use the same engine. Every message pays for all four parts, and
the record keeps each part's full context window, the regulator's stated
reading and decision, every introspector cell, and the per-cell match
verdicts.
